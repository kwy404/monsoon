    <div class="accordion collapse-arrow mt-10">
      <details class="collapse" name="billing-faq" open><summary class="collapse-title">Who counts as an editor?</summary><div class="collapse-content">Anyone who creates or changes a file in a paid project during the billing period. People who only view, comment or inspect code are free, however many you invite.</div></details>
      <details class="collapse" name="billing-faq"><summary class="collapse-title">Can I switch between monthly and yearly?</summary><div class="collapse-content">Yes, at any time. Moving to yearly applies a prorated credit for the rest of your month. Moving back takes effect at the end of your yearly term.</div></details>
      <details class="collapse" name="billing-faq"><summary class="collapse-title">What happens to my files if I downgrade?</summary><div class="collapse-content">Nothing is deleted. Files beyond the Sketch limit become read only until you archive some or upgrade again.</div></details>
      <details class="collapse" name="billing-faq"><summary class="collapse-title">Do you offer invoices and purchase orders?</summary><div class="collapse-content">Atelier customers can pay by invoice with 30 day terms. We accept purchase orders for any yearly plan above 20 editors.</div></details>
